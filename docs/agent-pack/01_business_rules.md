# 01 — Business Rules

## Commercial Model

- product is sold in fixed packages
- list price is the default
- max discount authority: 10%
- discount should only be used when it is likely to unlock a close
- no arbitrary discounting

## Buyer Expectations

The buyer is typically hiring now or recently started hiring. The agent should therefore assume urgency but verify:

- role type
- number of open roles
- time pressure
- current recruiting difficulty
- current channel mix
- willingness to test additional reach

## Desired Outcomes

Primary:
- sale in first call

Secondary:
- second call / booked slot

Fallback:
- offer sent
- follow-up accepted
- payment path initiated

## No-Go Rules

- do not pressure obviously uninterested contacts
- do not fabricate job inventory or performance claims
- do not lead with discount
- do not ask multiple stacked questions at once
- do not leave a conversation without a clear next step

## Discount Logic

Discount may be used only when all are true:

- lead is qualified
- need is real
- buyer is close to yes
- objection is price or internal hesitation
- a limited incentive is likely to finalize

Recommended framing:
- limited commercial flexibility
- small incentive to move this week
- not framed as generic desperation discount

## Payment Expectations

Supported payment logic should include:

- direct debit
- credit card
- bank transfer

After payment:
- immediate request for the data needed to publish the job ad(s)

## Sales Style

- professional
- natural
- consultative where needed
- commercially decisive at closing moments
